FROM THEORY TO CODE

What are you seeing?

1. Predict, then correct

The EKF predicts pose and covariance from the velocity command. A landmark inside sensor range and field of view supplies a noisy range and bearing measurement; its correction can shrink the position ellipse. Prediction and correction ellipses remain visible at about one-quarter intervals through the run. The plots compare the estimate with simulated ground truth and independent noisy odometry.

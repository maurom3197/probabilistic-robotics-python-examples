FROM THEORY TO CODE

How does the filter use measurements?

1. Predict, weight, and resample

Particles move under noisy velocity commands. Landmark range and bearing or laser endpoint likelihoods change their weights. When the effective particle count falls below half, the filter resamples. Each green 95% position ellipse reflects the weighted particle covariance after a sensor update; dashed gray ellipses show the prediction.
